    Wiring

    LED channels

    Each hexagon's M drives six chains. Hover one to pick it out.

    Each arrow runs from a channel's DIN to its DOUT, in the colour of the chain that drives it.

    Touch sensors

    Each node's sensor is wired, from the dot, to M or to T1, T2, T3 (collected at nodes 3, 6, 9); a ring is a node that takes its own. T1, T2 and T3 each go on to M as a twisted pair: 3 → 4 → M, 6 → 7 → M, 9 → 1 → M. Hover one to pick it out.

    Connectors

    A 2510 4-pin connector at each end of every channel, pins 1–4 in the same order at both ends.

    At a turn (E1–E3, or a neighbour's 3, 6, 9) a chain's DOUT SIG is jumped to pin 3 FREE of its partner channel, rides back past the LEDs, and is jumped to that channel's DIN SIG again at the inner node: the dotted rings. 5V and GND run through every connector. A coloured bar on the drawing marks a channel's IN end.
